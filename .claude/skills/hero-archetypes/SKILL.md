# 5 Hero Section Archetypes Skill (@ose.builds)

**Ban:** Never default to the forgettable SaaS template of "headline + subheadline + 2 buttons on the left, floating Tilted UI screenshot on the right." Instead, select the archetype that matches the industry and subject:

| # | Archetype | Structure & Visual Mechanics | Best Industries / Use Cases |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Split Hero** | Break the display headline into two parts and place the live product interactive widget, 3D object, or focal visual **inside the gap** so the product is literally part of the sentence. | Fintech, SaaS, developer tools, single-product brands |
| **2** | **Full-Bleed Hero** | Clean, structured editorial typography anchored at the top (or bottom edge) over a single breathtaking, edge-to-edge (`100vw × 100dvh`) photograph or video. Reads like an iconic magazine cover. | Hardware, architecture, luxury, automotive, physical products |
| **3** | **Orbit Hero** | High-impact headline and single CTA centered in the viewport, with curated real photos, UI micro-cards, or live artifacts floating in a balanced spatial orbit around it (with subtle parallax/spring drift). | Agencies, creative studios, clinics, real estate, community platforms |
| **4** | **Gallery Hero** | Frame or wrap the central headline inside a living wall, bento grid, or horizontal/angled ribbon of real work outputs and artifacts. Show volume and proof immediately. | AI tools, design studios, marketplaces, photography/video platforms |
| **5** | **Wordmark Hero** | Set the brand name at massive viewport-spanning scale (`15vw–24vw`, tight tracking `-0.04em`), with the hero product or live interactive card layered directly in front of the giant letterforms. | Fashion, events, consumer brands, creator/cultural products |
